How Arabic letters change shape

Arabic is written from right to left. Six letters — ا د ذ ر ز و — do not join to the following letter.

Introduction to Tajweed — أحكام التجويد

Tajweed teaches the correct pronunciation of Qur’anic letters and recitation rules.

1. Noon Sakinah and Tanween

Iz-har, Idgham, Iqlab and Ikhfa.

2. Meem Sakinah

Ikhfa Shafawi, Idgham Shafawi and Iz-har Shafawi.

3. Madd

Rules of lengthening during Qur’an recitation.

4. Qalqalah

The echoing quality of ق ط ب ج د when they carry sukoon.

5. Ghunnah

The nasal sound associated with نّ and مّ.

6. Waqf and Ibtida

Rules of stopping and beginning during recitation.

These are introductory explanations. A qualified Qur’an teacher should review the detailed lessons.
