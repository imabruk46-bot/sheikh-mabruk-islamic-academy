3. Tajweed Rules & Recitation Mechanics

Tajweed ensures the correct pronunciation of Quranic letters and attributes. Core topics covered in this module include:

  • Makharij al-Huroof: The articulation points of letters (Throat, Tongue, Lips, Nasal passage).
  • Sifat al-Huroof: Characteristics of letters (Whispered vs. Voiced, Heavy vs. Light).
  • Rules of Noon Sakinah & Tanween: Izhar (Clear), Idgham (Merging), Iqlab (Conversion), and Ikhfa (Concealment).
  • Rules of Meem Sakinah: Idgham Shafawi, Ikhfa Shafawi, and Izhar Shafawi.
  • Madd (Prolongation): Natural vowels and secondary elongation rules (Madd Lazim, Madd Muttasil, etc.).

4. Maliki Fiqh (Islamic Jurisprudence)

Detailed legal rulings according to the Maliki Madhab (based on foundational texts like Matn Ibn Ashir and Al-Risalah by Ibn Abi Zayd al-Qayrawani):

  • Kitab at-Taharah (Purification): Rules of Wudhu (Ablution), Ghusl (Full bath), Tayammum, and defining pure vs. impure substances (Tahir and Najis).
  • Kitab as-Salah (Prayer): Obligations (Fara'id), recommended acts (Sunan), invalidators of prayer, and how to make up missed prayers (Qada).
  • Kitab az-Zakah: Calculation of wealth thresholds (Nisab) on gold, silver, crops, and livestock.
  • Kitab as-Siyam (Fasting): Rulings of Ramadan, exemptions, and compensation (Kaffarah/Fidya).

5. Sirah (Prophetic Biography) & Aqidah (Creed)

The Blessed Sirah:

A structured study of the life of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH), moving from his birth in Makkah, the years of persecution, the migration (Hijrah) to Madinah, the establishment of the Islamic state, and the final farewell pilgrimage.

Aqidah & Tawhid:

Establishing correct Islamic beliefs based on the Quran and Sunnah:

  • Tawhid ar-Rububiyyah: Oneness of Lordship (belief that Allah is the sole Creator and Sustainer).
  • Tawhid al-Uluhiyyah: Oneness of Worship (directing all acts of worship solely to Allah).
  • Tawhid al-Asma was-Sifat: Belief in Allah's unique Names and Attributes without distortion or likeness to creation.
  • The Six Pillars of Iman: Belief in Allah, His Angels, His Books, His Messengers, the Last Day, and Divine Decree (Qadar).
