Tajweed – أحكام التجويد

Learn introductory Tajweed rules. These are original short learning notes, not a reproduction of a published book.

1. What is Tajweed?

Tajweed means giving each Arabic letter its proper pronunciation and characteristics when reciting the Qur'an.

2. Noon Saakin and Tanween

The main rules are Izhar, Idgham, Iqlab and Ikhfa.

  • Izhar: Clear pronunciation.
  • Idgham: Merging one sound into another.
  • Iqlab: Changing the noon sound to a concealed meem sound before ب.
  • Ikhfa: Concealing the noon sound with ghunnah.

3. Meem Saakin

The three main rules are Ikhfa Shafawi, Idgham Shafawi and Izhar Shafawi.

4. Ghunnah

Ghunnah is a nasal sound. It is especially associated with نّ and مّ.

5. Madd

Madd means lengthening a vowel sound. Its types have different durations depending on the rule and recitation.

6. Qalqalah

Qalqalah is a slight echoing or bouncing quality on the letters ق ط ب ج د when they carry sukoon.

7. Rules of Laam

Study the rules of Laam in the definite article (ال), including the solar and lunar letters.

8. Makharij al-Huruf

Makharij are the articulation points from which Arabic letters are pronounced. Practise with a qualified teacher to correct the sounds.

These notes are introductory. Please learn the exact rules, examples and recitation lengths from a qualified Tajweed teacher.
